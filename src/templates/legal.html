1. What this service is

TWITCH-Generator is a self-hosted tool that turns a Twitch VOD (video on demand) into short vertical clips. It is operated privately by {{ operator }} for their own Twitch content. Access to the tool itself is restricted to invited users with a login.

2. Data we process

  • Twitch VOD data. The public video, its title, and its public chat replay for the VOD being processed. Chat messages are used only to measure audience activity over time; they are stored on our server for the duration of processing and deleted together with the VOD's working files.
  • Transcripts and analysis. The VOD's audio is transcribed on our server. Transcript excerpts and sampled video frames are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to identify highlight moments. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy; we do not send account data of any user there.
  • Connected platform accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram). If the operator connects a platform account via OAuth, we store the access and refresh tokens issued by that platform on our server. They are used solely to upload clips to that same account on the operator's request. We read only the basic profile information needed to display which account is connected (e.g. username / display name). We do not read followers, messages, or any other account data.
  • Login and technical data. A session cookie for signed-in users, and ordinary server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) kept for troubleshooting.

3. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no analytics services.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties. Data leaves our server only to the services named above (Twitch, Anthropic, and a platform the operator has explicitly connected) and only for the purpose described.
  • No automatic posting. Uploads to a connected platform happen only when the operator triggers them.

4. Storage and retention

All data is stored on a server operated by {{ operator }} in the European Union. VOD working files (video, transcript, chat replay, frames) are kept until the operator deletes the VOD from the tool. Platform OAuth tokens are kept until the operator disconnects the account, which deletes them immediately. You can also revoke access at any time in the respective platform's settings (e.g. TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps and services).

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and you can object to its processing. Contact the operator{% if contact %} at {{ contact }}{% endif %}. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the current version.
